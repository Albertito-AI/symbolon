# Code of conduct

Discuss work respectfully and support criticism with evidence. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory abuse, publication of private information and deliberate
misrepresentation are unacceptable. Contributors may disagree on technical,
commercial and legal questions without personal attacks.

The maintainer may request revisions, remove abusive content or restrict
participation, recording reasons where privacy permits. Report sensitive conduct
concerns privately to the maintainer's verified GitHub contact route. Do not
publish personal information in an issue. This project does not promise a
dedicated investigation service or response-time commitment.
